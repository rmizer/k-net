K-NET: FEATURE LIST
K Network Project Center. Production coordination for large film shoots.
Written 2026-10-21 from the live site (knetwork on Railway). Updated 2026-10-24.

======================================================================
THE BIG PICTURE
======================================================================
- One web app for the whole production: schedules, tasks, crew, talent, locations, vendors,
  documents and finance. Everyone works from the same current information.
- Works in any browser, on a phone or a computer. Nothing to install.
- Four roles, each seeing only what it needs:
    Site Manager  : everything, plus account, role and system tools
    Director      : runs the production (staff, schedule, shows, finance, documents)
    Assistant     : the same permissions as a Director, except creating shows
    Staff         : their own schedule, tasks and paperwork, plus the shared crew info
- Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the menus.
- Slack connection: announcements, alerts and task messages go to Slack, with a link back
  to K-Net. Sensitive details (finance, emergency contacts) stay in the app.
- Branded in K Network colors (dark red, red, black).

======================================================================
GETTING PEOPLE ON BOARD
======================================================================
- Sign-up page: name, email, phone, a strong password and agreement to the privacy statement.
- Email verification: new accounts can't sign in until they click the emailed link, which
  works once and expires after 3 days. People added by a manager skip this step.
- Invite Crew page: a QR code and link to the sign-up page, which you can download, share
  or print.
- Starter task: every new account gets "Join the K-Net Slack" with the invite link. Marking
  it done hides the Slack reminder on their Staff Hub.
- Built-in protection: sign-up is rate-limited and has a hidden bot trap.

======================================================================
STAFF HUB (the home page everyone lands on)
======================================================================
- Weather for the production location.
- Announcements and alerts, posted by managers and also sent to Slack.
- Daily Rundown: the day's key items.
- Your Tasks: tasks assigned to you, with overdue flags.
- Staff Tasks: tasks assigned to your department.
- This week's schedule preview.
- Today's Crew: who is working today.
- Share K-Net: a QR code to invite more crew.
- Join Slack band, until the person has joined.

======================================================================
SCHEDULE
======================================================================
- Day, week and month views.
- Filters by staff member, department and influencer. Influencer events are shown in a
  different style.
- Staff see their own schedule. Managers see the whole crew.
- My Tasks: open tasks grouped by when they're due.
- Master Timeline: the full production timeline. Managers add and edit timeline entries.

======================================================================
DIRECTOR'S DASHBOARD (Directors, Assistants, Site Managers)
======================================================================
- Overview: the master staff list and your own task list.
- Departments: add, rename and delete departments, and check people in or out of each one.
- Scheduling: edit each person's week, with overlapping shifts flagged.
- Block scheduling: book the same hours over date ranges on chosen weekdays in one step
  (e.g. Oct 5 to 30, weekdays, 9 to 5). Each day can be edited on its own, or the whole
  block deleted at once.
- Task Management: assign tasks to a person or a whole department, with a priority and due
  date. Tasks show on their Staff Hub and can message them on Slack.
- Shows (Directors, Assistants and Site Managers view; only Directors and Site Managers
  create and edit). Each show has its own page with its details, people and the influencers
  on it, sorted by week:
    name, description, start date, shooting location
    status: Planning, Pre-production or Production
    number of seasons and weeks per season
    lead Director, other Directors and Department Heads, chosen with side-by-side pick lists
    link to the show's influencers on the roster
- Request removal: Directors and Assistants ask Site Managers to remove an account.

======================================================================
STAFF / CREW
======================================================================
- Active Crew: the people currently working the production.
- Directory: everyone's contact info, department, role and lodging. Emergency contacts are
  visible only to managers.
- Department heads: mark a person as head of a department. If the department is deleted,
  their tile shows "No department listed".
- CrewFinder (preview): a live map of crew locations from trackers or phones. Only Site
  Managers add trackers. This is shown as a future feature.
- Invite Crew: QR code sign-up (see above).

======================================================================
INFLUENCERS
======================================================================
- Influencer roster in a sortable, searchable table.
- Columns and filters: show, season, week, platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Kick,
  Twitch), status, agent, travel and next scheduled item.
- Battle House Live is loaded with every influencer placed in their current week (1 to 3).
- Each influencer has a profile page with:
    contact details and agent or manager
    travel, lodging and other needs
    travel and lodging bookings (flight, ground transport, hotel, train)
    upcoming schedule
    notes that managers write and everyone can read
- Managers add and edit influencers, including their show, season and week.

======================================================================
RESOURCES
======================================================================
- Locations: lodging, airports, shooting locations, production base and other places, shown
  on a map and as tiles. Currently set up for Miami.
- Resources Directory: vendors and services by category: catering, film equipment, rentals
  (including boat and yacht rentals), transportation, utilities, technical, stores, permits
  and film office, medical and safety, lodging. Currently set up with 17 Miami vendors.
- Directory: the staff directory (see Staff / Crew).
- Paperwork: where everyone sends receipts and documents to the office (see below).
- Social Media: the show's official links (battlehouselive.com, Instagram @battlehouselive).

======================================================================
RESOURCES: PAPERWORK (everyone)
======================================================================
- Submit a receipt (photo, scan or PDF, with amount, store and date) or upload a document
  (contracts, insurance, permits, vehicle documents, invoices, vouchers, miscellaneous).
- A confirmation appears as soon as it's sent, and the new entry is highlighted.
- My Submissions: everything you've sent, with type, date, amount and status, plus how much
  you're still waiting to be paid back.
- Receipt tracking: Submitted, then Viewed, then In process, then Processed. Documents show
  Sent, then Seen by the office.
- Edit or withdraw anything until the office has opened it.
- Everyone sees only their own submissions.

======================================================================
OPERATIONS: DOCUMENTS (Directors, Assistants, Site Managers)
======================================================================
- Every document and receipt, filed by type: insurance, permits, contracts, vehicle
  documents, invoices, vouchers, receipts and miscellaneous.
- "Expiring soon" warnings for permits, insurance and other dated paperwork.
- Managers move each receipt along and see who sent what.

======================================================================
DEPARTMENTS: FINANCE (Finance department, Directors, Assistants, Site Managers)
======================================================================
- Its own entry in the Departments menu. Other departments' heads can't see it.
- The Finance department can also open everyone's receipts, to reimburse them.
- Team: the Finance department's people, schedule and tasks.
- Overview:
    staff paychecks due, vendor payments due, scheduled auto drafts and new receipts
    finance calendar showing what is due, scheduled and received
- Payroll:
    each person's pay rate and paychecks
    receipts attached to a paycheck as reimbursements
    the next pay period worked out automatically
- Incoming: money received, with a "revenue to date" chart.
- Outgoing: payments, receipts awaiting reimbursement, scheduled auto drafts, and a form to
  record a one-off payment.
- Invoices: track invoices and record payment.
- Payment Options: company cards, bank accounts and digital accounts (PayPal, Venmo, Zelle).
  Only the last four digits are kept (five for American Express), never full numbers.

======================================================================
SITE MANAGER DASHBOARD (Site Managers only)
======================================================================
- Users and roles: add users and change anyone's role or department inline. Nobody can
  change their own role.
- Remove accounts. Removed accounts are deactivated and kept on record, so they can be
  restored.
- Removal requests from Directors and Assistants: approve or dismiss.
- Email verification queue: resend the link or mark someone verified.
- Slack status for every person (Connected, Joined, Not in Slack or Off), plus a one-click
  Slack check that matches everyone by email.
- Departments: add and delete.
- Dev Login: the admin back end.

======================================================================
FOR EVERYONE
======================================================================
- Account page: edit your details, upload a profile picture, change your password, change
  your email (confirmed by email) and turn Slack messages on or off.
- Contact: report a bug or technical issue (what happened, where, what you expected) to the
  Site Managers.
- Help: how-to articles (e.g. joining Slack, submitting receipts and documents from your phone).
- Trial page: what the trial includes and excludes, data privacy, feedback.
- Investor page: what K-Net replaces, a savings estimate, the offer and where it could go.

======================================================================
BEHIND THE SCENES
======================================================================
- Hosted on Railway with a PostgreSQL database and stored uploads.
- Automatic database updates on every deploy.
- Full data export and import (one bundle file) for moving or backing up a production.
- Loaders that set up a production city in one command (Miami locations, vendors and
  documents) and load the Battle House weeks. The call sheet loader repeats the shoot day
  over any date range (now Oct 9 to Nov 7).
- Automated tests on the server, plus type checking and linting on the app.
